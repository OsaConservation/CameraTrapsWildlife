6 Arboreal Bridges

Introduction: Roads fragment tropical rainforests and pose a significant threat to arboreal (canopy-dwelling) mammals, which rely on continuous forest cover to move, forage, and reproduce. In the Osa Peninsula, Costa Rica—a global biodiversity hotspot—an expanding road network disconnects protected areas and increases wildlife-vehicle collisions. To address this challenge, Osa Conservation initiated the Arboreal Wildlife Bridge Program in December 2020.
